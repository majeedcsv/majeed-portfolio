CASE STUDY

Sales Performance Dashboard

The business question

How has revenue changed over time, and which regions perform best and worst? The dashboard was built to answer both at a glance.

The dataset

The data contains 10,000 e-commerce orders placed between January 2021 and December 2024, with 26 columns. These include order date, customer segment, region, country, product category, quantity, discount, revenue, cost, profit and order status. Orders come from 4 regions, 18 countries, 4 customer segments and 5 product categories.

Tools and process

  • PivotTables summarised revenue by region, country and customer type, and counted orders by customer type.
  • Charts turned each summary into a visual: a monthly revenue trend line, bar charts comparing regions and product categories, and a donut (pie-style) chart of revenue by customer segment.
  • SUM and AVERAGE formulas produced the headline figures shown on the dashboard.
  • Slicers for region, country, customer segment and year let a viewer filter the dashboard.
  • Conditional formatting with colour scales highlighted higher and lower values for quick reading.

Key findings

  • Revenue grew from $0.51M in 2021 to a peak of $2.31M in 2023, then fell about 50% to $1.15M in 2024. Order numbers followed the same pattern.
  • Regular customers generated the most revenue: $2.72M, about 51% of the total.
  • The Middle East was the top region ($1.35M) and Europe the lowest ($1.27M), but the gap between them is small, about 6%.
  • Canada was the top country ($454,911), followed closely by the USA ($444,542) and Mexico ($431,677).
  • Electronics was the leading category at $3.38M, about 64% of total revenue.

Limitations and next steps

The revenue figures include every order status. About 27% of total revenue ($1.44M) comes from orders marked Returned or Cancelled, so the dashboard shows total order revenue, not net revenue. A useful next step would be to filter to Delivered orders and see whether the trend and rankings change. The 2024 decline would also be worth investigating further, since this dataset does not explain it.
